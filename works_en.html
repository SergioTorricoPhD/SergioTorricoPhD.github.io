Riemann Hypothesis for Projective Curves

Academic Year 2025/2026 | Excellence Program, Advisor: Prof. Federico Pellarin

The paper studies the arithmetic properties of curves over finite fields and the zeta function of a curve. Specifically, the work presents Enrico Bombieri's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis for curves, an approach that avoids the use of surface geometry to focus on the direct study of the function field of the curve, the Riemann-Roch theorem, and the analysis of the fixed points of the Frobenius endomorphism.

Coverings of Riemann Surfaces

Academic Year 2023/2024 | Bachelor's Thesis, Advisor: Prof. Gabriele Mondello.

This thesis provides a detailed description of Riemann surfaces and their finite branched coverings. The main purpose of the work is to establish a direct correspondence between Riemann surface coverings and meromorphic function field extensions, formally linking them to the fundamental concepts of Galois theory.

Gelfand Transform

July 2025

These notes analyze Banach algebras, the spectrum of elements, and the Gelfand-Mazur theorem. The text concludes with the formulation of the Gelfand Transform, defined as a homomorphism between a commutative unital Banach algebra and a subalgebra of continuous functions.
